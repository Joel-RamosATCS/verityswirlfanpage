The Story

Verity started as a simple idea: what if one person had a whole family of different moods? That idea turned into this fan page, and then it kept growing.

Each variety is named after a feeling. Some are helpful, some are dramatic, and one is just here to cause a little trouble.

The best part is that you get to choose your favorite. There is no wrong answer, but Curiosity is right.
